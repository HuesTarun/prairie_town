📘 Prairie Town — Simple User Manual

Game is paused while you read. Take your time!

1. Camera & Basic Controls

  • Pan Camera: Drag anywhere on the screen, use WASD / Arrow Keys, or click/drag the 90m Valley Radar Bar at top center.
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel, or click + / − in the bottom-right dock. Click ⌖ to recenter.
  • Pause / Speed: Press Space to pause anytime, or click ⏸ / 1x / 2x at the top-right.
  • Brush & Cancel: Hold Shift + Drag with Tunnel or Trim to paint multiple tiles; Right-click any dashed task tile to cancel & refund resources.

2. How to Build Your Colony (5 Easy Steps)

  1. Trim Tall Grass (✂️ Trim): Click tall grass near your hole so your Sentry spots sneaky Coyotes early.
  2. Dig Tunnels (⛏️ Tunnel): Click soil next to your starting tunnel to dig deeper and sideways (+4 Dirt each).
  3. Create Bernoulli Fresh Air: Pack a 🌋 Crater Mound on your first hole (blocks rain floods!), dig another tunnel up to a second surface exit, and build a ⛰️ Dome Mound. Air will visibly flow down the Dome and out the Crater!
  4. Carve Specialized Chambers:
    • 👂 Listen: Right next to an entrance shaft (dogs pause to listen for danger before exiting).
    • 🌾 Granary: Underground room to store extra Food for storms.
    • 🧹 Latrine: Side room to keep Colony Hygiene % high.
    • 🛏️ Nursery: Dig below the orange 1.5m Coyote Dig Limit line so pups grow safely!
  5. Expand via Tooth-Touch (🦷 Kin Check): Dig sideways until you touch a glowing dashed Neighbor Border, then click Kin Check (or click the border with Inspect) to welcome neighbor prairie dogs!

3. Predator Alarms (1st Paused, Then Timed!)

Your first alert pauses so you can read calmly. After that, every alert runs on a live timer! Match keys 1, 2, or 3:

  • 1 · High-Pitch Dive Chirp: Fast soaring 🦅 Hawk
  • 2 · Rhythmic Ground Bark: Stalking 🐺 Coyote (Short grass gives +3s; Tall grass = Ambush!)
  • 3 · Long Low Whistle: Tall striding 🥾 Human

4. Coterie Roles (Bottom-Right Bar)

  • 👁️ Sentry: Stands atop mounds to scan sightlines and spot predators early.
  • ⛏️ Digger: Excavates queued tunnels/rooms and builds Crater & Dome mounds.
  • 🌾 Forager: Gathers prairie seeds & dry grass on sunny days to refill Food.
  • ✂️ Gardener: Automatically trims grass near entrances and keeps rooms tidy.
