# PRAIRIE TOWN: Coterie & Burrow — User Manual

Welcome! You can also read this anytime inside the game by clicking the **📘 Manual** button at the top-right (or pressing **H** / **?**) — opening the manual automatically pauses the game so nothing runs away while you read.

---

## 1. Camera & Basic Controls
- **Pan Camera:** Click and drag anywhere on the world, press **WASD / Arrow Keys**, or click/drag the **90m Valley Radar Bar (Minimap)** at top center.
- **Zoom In / Out:** Scroll your mouse wheel, or click **+ / −** in the bottom-right dock. Click **⌖** to recenter on your burrow.
- **Pause / Speed:** Press **Space** or click **⏸ / 1x / 2x** in the top-right bar.
- **Brush & Cancel:** Hold **Shift + Left-Drag** with **Tunnel** or **Trim** to paint multiple tiles; **Right-click** any yellow dashed blueprint tile (or click it with Inspect) to cancel and refund resources.

---

## 2. How to Grow Your Colony (5 Easy Steps)
1. **Trim Tall Grass (`✂️ Trim` / `G`):** Click tall grass near your hole so your Sentry spots sneaky Coyotes early instead of getting ambushed.
2. **Carve Tunnels (`⛏️ Tunnel` / `T`):** Click underground soil next to your existing tunnel to dig deeper and sideways. Every dug tile gives **+4 Packed Dirt** (and tapping deep blue Aquifers creates permanent **Mineral Springs** for passive Hygiene & drainage!).
3. **Engineer Bernoulli Fresh Air:**
   - Click **🌋 Crater** (`C`) and click your first surface hole to build a tall flood-proof Crater Mound (blocks rainstorm flooding!).
   - Dig a second tunnel sideways and up to the surface, then place a **⛰️ Dome** (`D`) mound on that second exit.
   - Wind moving faster over the tall Crater pulls stale air out while drawing crisp fresh air in through the low Dome!
4. **Build Specialized Underground Rooms:**
   - **👂 Listening Chamber (`L`):** Build right beside an entrance shaft (`0.5–1.0m` deep). Dogs pause here before exiting to listen for danger.
   - **🌾 Granary (`F`):** Underground dry storage room (+45 Food capacity each) to survive storms.
   - **🧹 Latrine (`R`):** Side toilet room that keeps Colony Hygiene % high.
   - **🛏️ Nursery (`N`):** Must be carved **below the dashed orange `1.5m Coyote Dig Limit` line** (`1.5m–5.0m`). Boosts Pup births and safe growth!
5. **Expand via Tooth-Touch (`🦷 Kin Check` / `K`):** Dig sideways until your tunnel reaches a glowing dashed Neighbor Border wall, then click **Kin Check** (or click the border with Inspect) to welcome neighbor coteries into your town!

## 3. Predator Alarms (1st Paused, Then Live Timer!)
Your **first predator alert pauses** automatically so you can learn calmly. Every alert after that runs on a **live countdown timer** — watch the pulsing edge alerts (`◀ 🦅` / `🐺 ▶`) when threats approach from off-screen!
- Press **`1` — High-Pitch Dive Chirp** for a fast soaring **🦅 Hawk**
- Press **`2` — Rhythmic Ground Bark** for a stalking **🐺 Coyote** (Short grass gives +3s; Tall grass = Ambush!)
- Press **`3` — Long Low Whistle** for a striding **🥾 Human**

*(Also use **📣 Whistle** / **`B`** to manually recall prairie dogs underground during emergencies!)*

---

## 4. Coterie Roles, Skills & Autonomous Sanctuary Goal
- **Roles (Bottom-Right Bar):** Use **`−` / `+`** (or click any prairie dog) to assign **👁️ Sentry**, **⛏️ Digger**, **🌾 Forager**, and **✂️ Gardener** roles.
- **Achievements & Skill Points (`✨ Skills & Goal` / `U`):** Completing milestones awards **Skill Points (SP)** to upgrade **Dig & Build Crew**, **Burrow Blueprints** (more simultaneous work blocks), **Alert Sightlines & Auto-Defense** (Master Steward at Lv3 auto-answers alarms!), and **Vitality & Harvest**.
- **Final Goal — Autonomous Prairie Sanctuary:** Complete all 5 victory milestones (Bernoulli Fresh Air `>=75%` + Crater Mound, Grand Burrow with `2+` of each room type, Grass Risk `<=25%` + Owl Ally, all `6/6` Neighbor Coteries united, and `20+` Dogs at `75%+` Hygiene & Morale) so your town runs, farms, and defends itself automatically!

---

## 5. Saving & Exporting Progress
- Your town auto-saves to `localStorage` every 15 seconds.
- Open **⚙️ Settings** anytime to **Save Now**, **Copy / Export JSON**, **Paste / Import JSON**, or **Reset Colony**.
